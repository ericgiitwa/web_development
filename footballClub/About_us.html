Our Story

Football FC Association was founded with a vision to nurture football talent, promote sportsmanship, and create opportunities for young athletes within Nairobi County and beyond. What began as a community-driven initiative has grown into a respected football association committed to empowering players, coaches, and local teams through organized competitions, training programs, and community engagement. Our passion for football continues to inspire unity, discipline, and excellence among the youth we serve.

Where We Operate

Football FC Football Association operates across Nairobi County, Kenya, working closely with local communities, schools, football clubs, and sports stakeholders to develop grassroots football. Our activities are focused on creating inclusive football programs that reach young players in different neighborhoods, providing them with a platform to showcase their talent and pursue their dreams in football. Through tournaments, mentorship, and development initiatives, we continue to strengthen football culture within the county.

Milestones

Over the years, Football FC Football Association has achieved significant milestones in promoting grassroots football development. We have successfully organized competitive local tournaments, supported the growth of emerging football clubs, and helped identify talented players with the potential to compete at higher levels. Our association has also built strong community partnerships and expanded participation among youth, reinforcing our commitment to shaping the future of football in Nairobi County. As we continue to grow, we remain dedicated to excellence, unity, and the development of the next generation of football talent.
