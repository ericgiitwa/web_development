Coding

Imagine being able to speak to machines and tell them exactly what to do. That’s what coding is—a language that bridges human ideas and computer actions. From the apps we use to order food, to the systems that run airplanes, coding is behind the scenes making it all work. It’s like giving step-by-step instructions to a very fast, very precise assistant. The beauty of coding is that anyone can learn it. Whether you want to build a game, design a website, or solve a real-world problem, coding gives you the power to create. In a world driven by technology, it’s not just a technical skill—it’s a superpower.
